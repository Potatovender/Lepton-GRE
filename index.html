Why Lepton exists

Math at every pixel

The renderer evaluates your formulas across the image. Use them for colour, texture, and shape, from a simple curve to a cloud-filled sky.

Two ways to edit

Edit equations in visual blocks or work with the whole scene as text. Both views keep your formulas, folders, and comments together.

Write familiar maths

Type fractions, powers, and roots directly in the equation editor. Paste LaTeX from elsewhere, or copy your scene as Lepton text to share it.
